Privacy Policy

CoDi — Fashion AI  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026

CoDi ("the app", "we", "us") is a fashion styling app. You upload a photo of a clothing item, an on-device AI model analyzes it, and the app suggests outfit combinations and similar products to shop. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language and is meant to be honest about how the app actually works.

Privacy-first by design. The AI that analyzes your clothing photo runs on your device — your photo is not sent to a server to be classified. Cloud AI (for written style tips) receives only short text tags such as "beige hoodie, casual", never your photo. An optional fully-offline AI mode keeps style tips on your device as well.

2. How your photos are handled

  • On-device Clothing classification (category, color, season) runs locally on your phone using an embedded model. The image is processed on the device.
  • Stored only if saved A photo is uploaded to our storage only when you explicitly add the item to your closet. It is stored in a per-user folder so the app can show your wardrobe across sessions.
  • Not used to train models We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • Deletable Deleting a closet item removes its photo from storage.

3. AI features & what leaves your device

  • On-device analysis: clothing recognition — stays on device.
  • Cloud style tips (Google Gemini API): when generating a written style tip, the app sends only text attributes of the outfit (e.g. colors, categories, styles) — never your photo or personal identity.
  • Optional Offline AI: if you enable it, style tips are generated entirely on your device and nothing is sent to any server.

5. How we use your information

  • To analyze clothing and generate outfit recommendations.
  • To personalize suggestions based on your closet, feedback, and preferences.
  • To maintain your account and sync your data across sessions.
  • To operate free and premium tiers.
  • To understand which features and products are useful (aggregate, in-app analytics).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and closet data until you delete the item or your account. Deleting a closet item removes its photo. Deleting your account removes your profile and associated data from our active database.

7. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: view and edit your profile and closet in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual closet items, or request full account deletion (see contact below).
  • Offline mode: enable on-device AI to avoid sending any outfit data to the cloud.
  • Permissions: camera and photo access are requested only when you upload an item, and can be revoked anytime in your device settings.

If you are in Canada, you have rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data.

8. Children

CoDi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.
